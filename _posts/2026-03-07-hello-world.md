This site will cover various things about my technical adventure. I have been in tech for a long time, but I'm just
starting to write about my experiences. I hope things you find here may help. If nothing else, I hope they are a 
reminder to myself how far I have come, and how far I still have to go. 


My first piece of advise: Don't forget to enjoy the journey. The outcome is usually not as fun as all the breaking 
and fixing in the middle. 


Some things that will appear here:

- Lab setups
- Tool builds and why I built them
- A technique I have refined over time
- Something I spent three hours debugging that turned out to be one line
- Reasons why I almost quit tech and threw everything in the trash to be a goat farmer...but didn't

"The best technical writing documents what you actually did, not a sanitized version of it. Include the wrong turns." - Claude
